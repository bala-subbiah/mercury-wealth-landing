Mercury Wealth
Product Brief

The advisor’s cockpit for running a book of business — built for external asset managers (EAMs), private banks and brokerages in Hong Kong and Singapore.

Why Mercury exists

A relationship manager’s day is scattered across custodian portals, spreadsheets, a CRM and email. The consequences are familiar: no single live view of the book, portfolio drift discovered at quarter-end instead of the day it emerged, proposals assembled by hand in slides, and an approval trail spread across inboxes when compliance asks for it. Mercury replaces that patchwork with one system that can answer, at any moment: how is my book positioned, what needs my attention today, and where is every client decision in its lifecycle?

What Mercury is

Mercury is a front-office platform that unifies the three things an advisor actually does — monitor the book, advise the client, and execute with governance — on a single multi-custodian, multi-currency data spine covering households, accounts, portfolios, holdings and activity.

Key capabilities

  • Book-of-business cockpit — one live view of every household and account across custodians: AUM, flows, performance, asset-class and currency exposure, all in base currency with native amounts one click away.
  • Morning briefing & attention feed — the day starts with a ranked list of what genuinely needs action: drift breaches, concentration flags, maturing positions, pending approvals, upcoming reviews.
  • Mandate-aware portfolio monitoring — every portfolio tracked against its model and risk profile, for advisory and discretionary (DPM) mandates alike; drift and concentration surface as they emerge, not at review time.
  • Proposals with built-in governance — investment proposals drafted, versioned, discussed and approved in one place, with client sign-off (in-app or e-signature) and a complete audit timeline on every proposal.
  • From acceptance to execution — an accepted proposal becomes orders; execution status is tracked on the same record the client approved.
  • Full instrument coverage — equities, bonds with yield and duration analytics, funds and structured notes, across the venues and access routes typical of an Asian book.
  • Relationship workspace — tasks, meetings and interaction history, a prospect pipeline, and client documents alongside the portfolios they concern.
  • Audit by default — every material action lands on an immutable audit log; the compliance answer is a filter, not an archaeology project.

What makes it different

  • Book-first, not account-first. Custodian portals show accounts; Mercury shows the business — rolled up, comparable, and scoped to exactly what each advisor is entitled to see.
  • Governance is the workflow, not paperwork after it. The proposal the client accepted, the approval that authorised it and the trades that executed it are one linked record.
  • Built for Asia’s operating reality. Multi-custodian, multi-booking-centre and multi-currency by design — HKD/SGD/USD base reporting, cross-border tax-lot awareness, and consistent numbers because every derived figure is computed from base facts, never keyed in twice.
